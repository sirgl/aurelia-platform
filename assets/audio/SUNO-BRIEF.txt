Audio for the historical 4X: the Suno generation pack
12 October 2026. These are tasks and prompts; the audio files have not been created yet.

START WITH THIS
First M01, M02, M03: 2-3 candidates each. Pick the common musical character,
then M04-M06. In parallel S01/S02/S03 and U01/U02/U03.
The durations below are an artistic target, not a guarantee of the generator's exact output.
For music: Create, Instrumental. For effects: Create -> Sounds -> one-shot/loop.
Do not paste the name of a game or of a composer in place of a concrete description.
An original melody is needed. Save the full mix first; stems are useful later.

GENERAL CHARACTER
Human scale, discovery of the world, warm hope, calm dignity.
Acoustic instruments, plenty of air, moderate dynamics. No constant epic
push, loud hits, modern drum kit or vocals.
The music must survive an hour of planning without tiring by repetition.

M01 - MAIN THEME, target 2-3 minutes, 2-3 candidates
Instrumental original theme for a historical turn-based strategy game. A warm
memorable four-note motif, sense of discovery and a civilization beginning.
72 BPM, modal harmony, intimate strings, wooden flute, plucked lyre, soft frame
drum, restrained French horn near the middle. Start gently, build one modest
hopeful crest, return to a quiet open ending. Elegant, human, spacious, organic.
No vocals, no choir, no trailer booms, no EDM, no rock drums, no huge finale.

M02 - MAP: EARLY MORNING, target 3-4 minutes
Instrumental background exploration music for a slow historical strategy game.
64 BPM, warm wooden flute fragments, soft plucked strings, low sustained cello,
occasional quiet frame drum. Sunlit river valley, patient curiosity, pastoral
calm. Sparse evolving arrangement, no verse-chorus form, no dominant repeated
hook, no dramatic climax. Gentle continuous texture suitable for long play.
No vocals, no choir, no environmental recordings, no abrupt ending.

M03 - MAP: DISTANT LANDS, target 3-4 minutes
Instrumental historical exploration underscore. 68 BPM, open modal harmony,
soft harp, breathy wooden flute, viola and cello, tiny scattered hand percussion.
Wonder at distant coastlines and unknown mountain passes, quietly optimistic.
Vary short melodic fragments with generous rests. Stable low energy, spacious
mix, no big crescendo, no vocals, no choir, no modern synth beat, no final hit.

M04 - MAP: PROSPERITY, target 3-4 minutes
Instrumental chamber music for a thriving historical city in a strategy game.
76 BPM, light pizzicato strings, gentle lute, lyrical clarinet, warm low strings.
Craftsmanship, harvest, cooperation, dignified optimism. Flowing and understated,
not a festival dance. Small melodic developments, quiet endings of phrases.
No vocals, no crowd sounds, no brass fanfare, no epic percussion, no huge climax.

M05 - TENSION, target 2-3 minutes
Instrumental restrained strategic tension underscore. 70 BPM, muted low strings,
sparse deep frame drums, dry plucked notes, distant low horn, suspended harmony.
A border dispute and uncertain diplomacy, patient caution rather than panic.
Steady low energy, no action movie climax, no sharp stingers, no vocals or choir.

M06 - WAR, target 2-3 minutes
Instrumental historical campaign music for deliberate turn-based decisions.
88 BPM, measured frame drums, low strings, restrained horns, occasional bowed
string ostinato. Resolve, danger and human cost, no triumphant constant march.
Moderate energy and clear breathing room. No trailer impacts, no machine-gun
percussion, no electric guitars, no vocals, no choir, no sudden final hit.

EXTRA MUSIC TASKS AFTER THE THEME IS CHOSEN
M07: diplomacy - a calm short variant of the main motif, 60-90 seconds.
M08: victory - a solemn soft resolution, 45-60 seconds.
M09: defeat - a restrained sad resolution without mockery, 30-45 seconds.
Later, for each nation: one recognisable theme and its peaceful/tense
arrangements. Do not generate a separate song for every unit, building or
technology: they need short sound families and events.

AMBIENCE: SOUNDS, LOOP
Initial targets 15-30 seconds; if the mode returns less, pick a good
fragment and build a longer loop when editing. Each environment separately.

UNITS: EFFECT SETS, NOT MUSIC
F01: one step of a leather boot on earth, 4-6 variants.
F02: one step on stone, 4-6 variants.
F03: one hoof touch on earth, 4-6 variants.
F04: a soft short creak of leather/gear, 3 variants.
F05: a single sword swing + separately a shield contact, 3 variants each.
F06: a bowstring release + separately an arrow hit, 3 variants each.
F07: an oar movement + separately a wooden ship hull, 3 variants each.

Template: Single [action and material], isolated dry close foley, short natural
decay, no music, no voice, no repeated sequence, no background ambience.
Tie the editing of steps and attacks to the actions/animation; constantly repeated
idle army sounds are not needed. Speech of units and leaders is a separate localisable project.

ACCEPTANCE AND USE
1. Listen, discard vocals/hum/artefacts, agree the timbre of the first three tracks.
2. Keep the original in the highest available quality, the Suno link/ID,
   the exact prompt, the date and the subscription terms. Do not publish tracks automatically.
3. A loop needs its seam checked; the word "loop" in the request does not guarantee a seamless one.
   Trim the fragment in an editor and add a short equal-power crossfade.
4. Do NOT treat independent generations as synchronised stems. For adaptive music
   take the parts of one source, or align tempo, length and phase by hand.
5. In the game keep one music bus, two crossfade slots, a limited pool of
   spatial ambient/foley voices, separate UI/Music/Ambience levels.
   Do not start a separate loop per hex. From the visible area pick
   a few representative sources of sea, river, forest and town.
6. A city or research screen over the map does not restart the music. Short cues
   do not stack in a bunch: limit repeats and duck the background slightly under an event.
7. Stream long music, cache short effects; the offline pack
   holds the ID, gain, loop points, variants, categories and limits of simultaneous voices.
